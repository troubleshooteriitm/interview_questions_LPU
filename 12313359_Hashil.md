### Answer

- More data
- Cross validation
- Regularization
- Early stopping

# 22. Explain Precision.

### Answer
Precision = TP / (TP + FP)

Measures how many predicted positives are actually correct.

---

# 23. Explain Recall.

### Answer
Recall = TP / (TP + FN)

Measures how many actual positives are detected.

---

# 24. What is F1 Score?

### Answer
The harmonic mean of Precision and Recall.

---

# 25. What is Confusion Matrix?

### Answer

- TP

- FN
