## Section 3: NYPD Data Analysis Project (26-45)
> **Context:** Your resume highlights experience cleaning data, engineering features, and creating Tableau dashboards.

26. Explain this project.
27. What dataset did you use?
28. Where did you download it?
29. How large was the dataset?
30. What was the objective?
31. Why use Pandas?
32. What cleaning steps did you perform?
33. What missing values existed?
34. How did you remove duplicates?
35. Why perform feature engineering?
36. Which new features did you create?
37. Why use datetime?
38. Explain one visualization you built.
39. Why choose Tableau instead of Power BI?
40. What business insights did your dashboard provide?
41. What would happen if timestamps were missing?
42. What KPIs did you track?
43. How would you improve this dashboard?
44. What role did Git play?
45. What was your individual contribution?

## Section 5: AI Fitness Trainer (61-75)
> **Context:** The AI Fitness Trainer is likely the project interviewers will spend the most time discussing because it combines AI, OpenCV, and Deep Learning.

61. Explain your AI Fitness Trainer.
62. What problem does it solve?
63. Why build this project?
64. How does the user interact with it?
65. Why OpenCV?
66. What is MediaPipe?
67. How does pose detection work?
68. How do you count repetitions?
69. Which deep learning model are you using?
70. How accurate is it?
71. How do you personalize workout plans?
72. How do you generate diet plans?
73. How does adaptive scheduling work?
74. If MediaPipe fails to detect joints, what happens?
75. If you had ₹1 crore to improve it, what would you add?
